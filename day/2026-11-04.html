1. Future leaders often reveal themselves during corrections.

4. Let the market earn your exposure.

5. A follow-through day is important, but not a guarantee.

A follow-through day signals a potential change in market direction. However, not every follow-through day leads to a sustained uptrend. Watch whether leading stocks break out successfully, market breadth improves, and institutional buying supports the recovery.

Market corrections aren't a reason to stop doing your homework. They're an opportunity to identify potential leaders, study their fundamentals, and monitor their technical strength.
