An Amazon memo details strategy for agentic AI shopping, per Reuters.

It currently blocks AI agents from platform. Plans to build a “World Product Graph”, basically a search engine for every product on Earth (even ones Amazon doesn't sell).

The memo writes that “whoever owns the product data owns the agent."

Currently, 57% of shoppers starting product research begin on Google (vs. 16%). However, if shoppers know exactly what they want, then 41% start on Amazon (vs. 23% on Google).

A “World Product Graph” also meant to address shortcomings of Amazon’s Rufus chatbot:

▫️”Many Rufus answers are factually wrong or incomplete, but customers lack expertise to detect these errors.”
▫️Rufus did about 83M searches a week, and the memo calls running them “expensive”.

One made-up customer quote explains problem trying to be solved: “I used to spend an hour on Reddit figuring this stuff out.”
