what is product management, if we break it down to first principles

there is an organization that exists to create value for the market it serves.

to create real value for a market, someone has to figure out what that market needs and design something that solves that need.

the solution must be something the organization can actually build and sell.

it must not conflict with the current strategy. ideally, it should clearly fit the strategy, strengthen it, or even help shape and define the strategy.

- it’s not linear; you take quite a few shots. good stuff takes time.
- it’s heavy on execution and grind, but it also has to be creative and maintain trust.
- experience and product sense matter a lot.
- work used to be done by many people. now maybe not so much, but it still requires others. someone has to lead it.

this function is key in an org, and i still think 1 orchestrator is best here (human or AI, tbc)

otherwise each function pushes into its own corner, and you never reach the bare equilibrium you need to be successful. the product has the wrong components, or gets shipped too late.

(bad product management can happen too. one person, but the wrong one, and you get the same result.)

who should be doing this role?

technically, anyone... assuming they are high-agency, analytical, creative, clear thinker and communicator, low ego, high trust and integrity, work well with others, and ideally have lots of good ideas, experience and/or at least talk to customers all day.
